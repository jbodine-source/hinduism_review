ahimsa - nonviolence dharma - good living, following the right or proper path karma - the belief that one's actions, good or bad, will return to the person (in this life or the "next") samsara - the cycle of birth, death, and reincarnation puja - daily acts of worship Vedas - some of the texts sacred to Hindus guru - a spiritual teacher, one who completely understands Hinduism moksha - to be free from samsara, to become one with Brahman after death reincarnation - the belief that one's soul will enter a new physical body after death caste system - a strict social hierarchy
